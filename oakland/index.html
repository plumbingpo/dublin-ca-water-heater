Keeping Your Oakland Water Heater Running Longer

A little routine care makes all the difference between a water heater that runs for years and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: an annual sediment flush, an anode inspection every few years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a temperature setting around 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so small issues get caught early.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms such as rusty hot water, rumbling during heating, moisture around the base, or hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one with water on the floor.

The sooner you call about a hot water problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A tank that's just begun to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet the same sediment, neglected through a few more years, can create hot spots on the tank floor and burn out the lower element on an electric model, forcing a replacement much sooner. Save our number on your phone today so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or looks wrong. A quick call saves time, money, and ruined floors.
